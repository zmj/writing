# Babel

Developer discourse is weird these days. Comments about [dark factories](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/7/software-factory/) and dozens of concurrent terminal agents sit side by side with reports of frontier agents being unable to produce useful code. The acceleration in capabilities paired with uneven diffusion results in wildly different experiences on the same calendar day.

Diffusion is multi-dimensional: tech stack, codebase verifiability, development workflow, and (uncomfortably) writing skill all affect how much of a capability gain can be captured. Distances are large in high dimensional spaces. The future is already here, but it's very unevenly distributed.

The metaphor that comes to mind is a reversed Tower of Babel. Developers can no longer usefully exchange experiences; not because the tower fell, but because we're on different floors, and the tower is growing exponentially taller.
